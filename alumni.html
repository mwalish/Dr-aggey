Alumni & Community

"Doc Boys" — leaders across every field

Generations of Dr. Aggrey students — affectionately known as "Doc" or "Doc Boys" — have gone on to lead in the judiciary, media, sport, faith, and public office. Their achievements are a source of pride for the entire Wundanyi and Taita Taveta community.

Alumni Association

A formal, published "Old Boys Association" contact was not found in public records for this school. Given the strength of the alumni above — including a retired High Court Judge and respected national media figures — we recommend the school formally establish (or publicise, if one already exists) an Alumni Association, with a designated contact person, to support mentorship, fundraising for facility rebuilding, and scholarship opportunities for current students.

Community Connection

Dr. Aggrey is deeply intertwined with the local Wundanyi community. The school has reported running a "Community Class" initiative that uses affirmative action to admit talented boys from local primary schools who may have narrowly missed the standard national cut-off scores — a model of inclusive access in a historically marginalized county.
