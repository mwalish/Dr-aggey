Admissions for Grade 10 (Senior School)

Contact Admissions Office

We welcome new learners to join Dr. Aggrey Senior School! Whether you're joining Grade 10 through national placement or applying to transfer, we aim to make the admission process simple and transparent.

Entry Requirements

Grade 10 (Senior School)

Placement is coordinated centrally by the Ministry of Education. Grade 9 learners select Dr. Aggrey Senior School online during the national placement exercise, and admission is confirmed against available vacancies and the learner's chosen pathway (STEM, Social Sciences, or Arts & Sports Science).

Pathway Selection

Learners indicate their preferred pathway during national selection. Our academic team supports incoming learners with guidance on subject combinations at the start of Term 1.

Transfer Students

Considered on a vacancy basis and subject to meeting subject/pathway prerequisites. Please contact the Principal's office directly to discuss a transfer.

How to Apply

1

Select the School During National Placement

Grade 9 learners select Dr. Aggrey Senior School as a choice during the official Ministry of Education / KUCCPS online placement exercise.

2

Report with Required Documents

  • Official placement/admission letter
  • KJSEA / KCPE results slip (or transfer letter, if applicable)
  • Copy of birth certificate
  • 4 recent passport-sized photos
  • Copy of parent/guardian national ID
3

Clear Fees at Reporting

Fees are payable via the school's official bank account or M-Pesa Paybill on reporting day — see the Fee Structure below. Please confirm official payment channels with the school office before sending any payment.

Fee Structure (2026 Senior School Guideline)

As a National (Cluster C1) boarding school, Dr. Aggrey Senior School follows the Ministry of Education's standardised Senior School fee guideline, gazetted 4 November 2025 (Gazette Notice No. 1555):

  • Annual Boarding FeeKsh 53,554
  • Government Capitation (per learner)Ksh 22,244
  • Maintenance & Improvement Fund (GoK)Ksh 5,000
  • Parent Contribution to M&I FundKsh 2,000

By law, no student may be sent home for boarding-fee arrears (Basic Education Act, 2013).

Schools may not charge above the gazetted rate or add levies without written Ministry approval through the County Education Board.

Figures reflect national Ministry of Education guidance for all Cluster C1 (former National) public boarding Senior Schools as of the 2026 academic year. The school's admissions office can confirm the current application/registration fee and official payment details.

Boarding

Dr. Aggrey Senior School is a Boys' Boarding school. Boarding spaces follow national placement allocation. Please contact the school office to confirm current dormitory arrangements and any day-scholar provisions.
