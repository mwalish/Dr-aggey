Our Story

History & Inspiration: Dr. Aggrey Senior School was established in Wundanyi in the years following Kenya's independence, in what was then a marginalized part of the former Coast Province. The school is named in honour of Dr. James Emman Kwegyir Aggrey (1875–1927), a US-trained Ghanaian missionary and educationist celebrated for his belief that "nothing but the best is good enough for Africa," and for his role in co-founding Achimota College in Ghana. Generations of students here have affectionately been known as "Doctarians" or "The might docta"

National School Elevation: In a historic milestone on 26 July 2025, the institution was officially elevated to National School status, in recognition of its stellar trajectory in both sports and academic performance — a moment celebrated with a public inauguration ceremony attended by area leaders.

Enrolment & Structure: The school hosts a student population of roughly 2,000 students. Under Kenya's transition to the Competency-Based Curriculum (CBC), it operates as a Senior School catering to Grades 10, 11, and 12, offering the STEM, Social Sciences, and Arts & Sports Science pathways. Its official KNEC examination code is 1101101 (UIC: FV6C).

What Sets Us Apart

A National School with Local Roots

Elevated to National status in July 2025 while remaining deeply rooted in the Wundanyi and wider Taita Taveta community that has supported the school for decades.

Distinguished Alumni

Our old boys include a retired High Court Judge, respected national broadcasters, a professional rugby international, and elected community leaders. See our Alumni page.

Vibrant Community

Dr. Aggrey is deeply intertwined with the local Wundanyi community. The school runs a "Community Class" initiative that uses affirmative action to admit talented boys from local primary schools who may have narrowly missed standard national cut-off scores.

Holistic Development

We balance strict academic performance with character development and high-level co-curricular achievement, particularly in sport — one of the two pillars that earned our National School status.

Rebuilding & Renewal

Like many boarding institutions nationally, we have faced recent facility setbacks. The school administration and Board of Management are actively rebuilding affected dormitories and strengthening campus safety systems. See Recent Developments.

Discipline and Accountability

Discipline has long been treated as the foundation for success in both academics and extracurricular life at Dr. Aggrey.

At Dr. Aggrey Senior School, we are committed to providing a nurturing environment that fosters academic excellence and personal growth — developing the whole child, so that every learner grows into a responsible and compassionate adult.
