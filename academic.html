Academics at Dr. Aggrey

Knowledge | Skills | Excellence

Dr. Aggrey Senior School delivers the Competency Based Curriculum (CBC) at Senior School level, ensuring learners build skills, values, and knowledge for the 21st century.

Senior School – Grades 10, 11, 12

Career Pathways Offered

STEM

Sciences, Mathematics & Technology

Social Sciences

History, Geography, Business, Languages

Arts & Sports Science

Music, Art, Drama, Physical Education

Every learner takes 7 subjects: 4 core subjects — English, Kiswahili/Kenya Sign Language, Mathematics, and Community Service Learning — plus 3 subjects from their chosen pathway.

Exam & Revision Support

  • Structured revision programmes and mock examinations
  • Past paper practice and targeted remedial sessions
  • Career guidance and university/college placement support
  • Block timetabling with dedicated revision periods

Teaching & Support

Qualified Teachers

TSC-registered staff trained for the CBC Senior School curriculum

Modern Facilities

Science labs, Computer lab & Library — see our Facilities page

Mentorship

Academic clinics & student support from staff and alumni

Progress Reports
