As a Boarding National School, Dr. Aggrey Senior School's campus supports both academic and residential life for roughly 2,000 learners. The list below reflects the facilities typically expected of a Cluster C1 boarding institution; the school administration should confirm exact counts, capacities, and recent upgrades so this page can be published with precise figures.

Classrooms

Dedicated classroom blocks for Grades 10–12 across all three CBC pathways.

Library

A reading and reference resource supporting independent study and exam preparation.

Science Laboratories

Physics, Chemistry and Biology labs supporting the STEM pathway.

ICT Laboratory

Computer studies and digital literacy for CBC learners.

Boarding Dormitories

Residential accommodation for our Boys' boarding community.

Dining Hall

Central dining facility serving the boarding student population.

Sick Bay / Medical

On-site first-response medical care for boarding learners.

Sports Grounds

Playing fields supporting the sporting excellence that helped earn our National School status.

Chapel / Assembly Hall

A shared space for assemblies, worship, and school-wide gatherings.

Dr. Aggrey Senior School

A National School in Wundanyi, Taita Taveta County. Elimu na Maendeleo — Education and Development.
