Achievements & Extracurricular

Excellence beyond the classroom

Our Proudest Achievement

Sporting excellence was one of the two pillars — alongside academic performance — explicitly cited when Dr. Aggrey was elevated to National School status on 26 July 2025. Our sporting programme is a core part of the school's identity.

Notable Alumni Achievement in Sport

Willy Ambaka — International Rugby

An old boy of Dr. Aggrey, Willy Ambaka went on to play rugby at international level, including a professional career in South Africa — a source of pride for the school and a role model for current athletes.
