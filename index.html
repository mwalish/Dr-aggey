About Our School

Dr. Aggrey Senior School sits about 200 metres from Wundanyi town in the scenic Taita Hills. Named after the pioneering Ghanaian educationist Dr. James Emman Kwegyir Aggrey, the school has grown from a county institution into a National School, currently home to roughly 2,000 boys across Grades 10–12 under Kenya's Competency-Based Curriculum.
